Who am I ?

Hi, I'm Harsh, and I've always been that kid curious about how things work—especially when it comes to technology. Back in 11th grade, I took Information and Technology as a subject, and that's where I met Java. It was love at first sight! I was hooked on coding, but life had other plans. I ended up pursuing a BTech in Civil Engineering (yeah, I know, a bit of a plot twist). After that, I worked as a site engineer and started preparing for government exams. Then, COVID hit, and like many others, I had to make some tough decisions. I left my job and focused on clearing the GATE exam, which led to my selection for an MTech in Structural Engineering at IIT. But despite my academic achievements, the itch for technology never left me. Coding, tech, and the digital world were where my heart truly belonged.So, three months into my IIT journey,I made the bold decision to leave and dive headfirst into data science. It was a leap of faith, but as luck would have it, my favorite teacher (who I'd followed on YouTube) started his first-ever online data science batch at just the right time. I joined, and it felt like everything clicked into place again. Learning to code felt like rediscovering an old passion, and I couldn't be more grateful to my teachers and parents for supporting me through it all. Now, I'm living my dream, coding away, and loving every second of it. Here's to following your passion, no matter where life takes you!.
